Road Runner Mini is a small robot with two legs and a wheel at the end of each. It balances on the wheels like an inverted pendulum, drives and turns from a keyboard or gamepad, and raises or lowers itself by bending its five-bar legs. A neural-network policy trained with reinforcement learning in MuJoCo runs on the Raspberry Pi 5, and an OAK-D Lite depth camera in the head lets it slow down for obstacles and steer around them.
